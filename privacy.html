FITDASH Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 · Applies to the FitDash web app (sazid-abdullah.github.io/Fit-Dash) and the FitDash Android app (com.sazidabdullah.fitdash).

Health & medical disclaimer. FitDash is a fitness and nutrition tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or condition. Calorie targets, body-fat estimates, training suggestions and AI Coach replies are estimates for general wellness only and may be wrong. Consult a doctor or qualified professional before starting a new diet or exercise program, especially if you are pregnant, injured, under 18, or have a medical condition. Stop exercising immediately if you feel pain, dizziness, or shortness of breath.

1. Summary

  • FitDash works without an account. By default all your data stays on your device.
  • If you choose to sign in, a copy of your fitness data is stored in Google Firebase so you can sync between devices. Only you can access it.
  • Your AI provider API keys never leave your device.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.
  • You can delete your account and all data from inside the app at any time.

2. Data stored on your device

FitDash saves what you enter in your browser's or the app's local storage (localStorage) on your device:

CategoryExamples
TrainingWorkout sessions, sets, reps, weights, personal records, custom exercises, routines, cardio sessions
NutritionFood and calorie logs, water intake, meal plans, saved AI plans, custom dishes, grocery lists
Body & wellnessBody weight, body composition, body circumference, sleep, daily checklist
Profile & settingsHeight, age, gender, activity level, goals, schedule, reminders, theme
AI CoachChat history, chosen provider and model, and your API key (only if you choose to store it)

This data is not sent anywhere unless you turn on cloud sync or use the AI Coach as described below. Clearing your browser/app data or using Settings → Data Management → Clear All Data removes it.

3. Optional account & cloud sync (Firebase)

Signing in is optional. If you create an account with email/password or Google Sign-In, FitDash uses Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Authentication stores your email address, display name and profile photo (for Google sign-in), and a unique user ID.
  • Cloud Firestore stores one document, users/{your user ID}/fitdash/state, containing the training, nutrition, body, profile and goal data listed above so it can be restored on your other devices.
  • Security rules only allow the signed-in owner of an account to read or write that document.
  • Never uploaded: your AI Coach configuration and API keys, AI chat history, and app settings that only matter on one device.

Firebase is operated by Google LLC. See the Firebase privacy information and Google Privacy Policy. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by Google.

4. AI Coach

The AI Coach is optional and uses your own API key for the provider you choose (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, or a local Ollama server). When you send a message, FitDash sends it directly from your device to that provider, along with recent chat messages and, if Share health data with AI is turned on in Settings, a summary of your profile, goals and recent progress. FitDash has no server in between and never sees your API key. The provider's own privacy policy applies to those requests:

  • Groq · Google Gemini · OpenRouter · Ollama runs on your own computer.

Do not enter information in the AI Coach that you would not want shared with your chosen provider.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): for workout, water and meal reminders you set up.
  • Keep screen awake: during an active workout so the timer stays visible. Released when the workout ends.
  • Internet: only for optional cloud sync, sign-in and the AI Coach.

6. Deleting your data and account

  • With an account: open Settings → Account & Cloud Sync → 🗑️ Delete Account & All Cloud/Local Data. This permanently deletes your Firestore document, your Firebase Authentication account, and all FitDash data on that device. You may be asked to sign in again to confirm.
  • Without an account: Settings → Data Management → Clear All Data, or clear the app's storage in your browser/Android settings.
  • If you can't access the app, open an issue at github.com/sazid-abdullah/Fit-Dash/issues asking for account deletion (don't post personal data publicly; we will contact you to verify the request). We will delete the account and its cloud data once the request is verified.

7. Children

FitDash is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data retention

Local data stays until you delete it. Cloud data stays while your account exists and is deleted when you delete your account.

9. Changes

We will update this page and its effective date if this policy changes. Significant changes will be shown in the app.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: github.com/sazid-abdullah/Fit-Dash/issues.
