Satiety Tips
Protein + volume first: fill half the plate with vegetables (lau, cabbage, cauliflower, shak, salad), a quarter with lean protein, then add rice, ruti or potato to fit your calories.
Track the trend, not the day: weigh in most mornings and compare weekly averages. Losing 0.25–1% of body weight per week on a cut is on track; if you're losing faster, add a little food.
Cook low-oil: 1 tsp of oil is ~45 kcal. Measure it, steam or bhuna with a splash of water, and use non-stick pans or an air fryer.
Smart extras: plain strained doi, clear dal soup, cucumber-tomato salad, muri, guava, papaya and black tea or coffee add volume for very few calories.

FitDash User Guide

🏋️‍♂️ Training & Workouts

- Workout Blocks: Workouts are split into blocks (e.g. A, B, C). Exercises inside a block are done as a circuit or superset. Finish all rounds of a block before moving to the next.
- Custom Exercises: Add your own exercises from the Training tab. They appear in a "Custom Block" at the bottom of your workout.
- Moving Exercises: Click the "Move" button next to any exercise to permanently shift it to another block.
- Live Tracking: Tap the workout button on the dashboard to start a live session with automatic rest timers.

🥗 Nutrition & AI

- Food Logging: Use the Nutrition tab to log foods. You can enter natural text like "I ate 200g rice and 100g chicken" and the AI will parse it (requires AI setup).
- Macro Calculations: Your daily calorie and macro goals are automatically calculated based on your height, weight, and goals in Settings.
- Photo Scanner: Take a picture of a nutrition label, and the AI will automatically log the macros.
- AI Chat: Click the floating robot icon to chat with your AI coach. It knows your recent workouts, PRs, and food logs.

⚙️ Setup & Syncing

- AI Setup: Go to Settings -> AI Config to add a free API key (like Groq or Google Gemini). This powers the smart food logging and chat.
- Offline Support: This app works 100% offline. All data is saved safely in your browser's local storage.
- Data Backup: In Settings, use "Export Data" regularly to download your history, since clearing your browser cache could delete your progress.

🔒 Privacy Policy & Health Disclaimer
⚕️ Not a medical device. FitDash is a fitness and nutrition tracking tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and its calorie, body-fat, training and AI Coach suggestions are estimates for general wellness only. Talk to a doctor or qualified professional before starting a new diet or exercise program, especially if you are pregnant, injured, or have a medical condition. Stop exercising if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.

What FitDash stores

Workouts, PRs, food and calorie logs, weight, body measurements, water, sleep, cardio, goals and settings. By default all of it is stored only in your browser/device storage (localStorage). We have no server that collects it.

Optional cloud sync

If you sign in (Google or email), a copy of that data is saved in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) under your account so you can restore it on other devices. Only you can read it. Your AI provider API keys are never uploaded.

AI Coach

When you use the AI Coach, your message (and, if you allow it in Settings, a summary of your profile and progress) is sent directly from your device to the AI provider you chose (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, or your own Ollama) using your own API key. Their privacy policies apply to those requests.

Deleting your data

Settings → Account & Cloud Sync → Delete Account & All Cloud/Local Data permanently deletes your cloud data, your sign-in account and the data on this device. Without an account, Settings → Data Management → Clear All Data removes everything stored on the device.
