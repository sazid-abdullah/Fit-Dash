Diet Tips
Weigh yourself 5–7×/week in the morning. Track the weekly median (middle number), not the daily reading. First week may show water weight loss — that's normal.
On track: losing 0.25–1% of body weight/week → don't change anything. Losing faster? Eat more. Not losing? Reduce snacks, shrink one meal, or add 50 min cardio/week.
Off-plan meals: stay within the calorie budget for that slot. Up to 450 cal (1500 plan), 600 cal (2000 plan) or 750 cal (2500 plan) swap is fine. Bigger meal? Skip the nearest meal and optional snacks.
🇧🇩 BD low-density hacks: Plain Arong/Danish doi (unlimited) · boiled fish without oil · steamed vegetables · clear dal soup · cucumber & tomato salad · diet water (plain) · sugar-free Horlicks or Bournvita if craving something sweet.
Full Body · Home Gym

Full Body Protocol

📅 2× per week
⏱ ~55–70 min
🔋 3 Sets per Block
🚶
Pre-Workout Warmup
Brisk walk or light jog in place. Moderate pace — break a light sweat, warm the joints before loading.
5–10 min
A
Legs & Calves — Superset
A1 → rest → A2 → rest. That's one set. Complete all 3 sets before moving on.
Superset
A1Dumbbell Goblet Squat
A2Standing Calf Raises
B
Back · Chest · Back — Giant Set
B1 → B2 → B3 back-to-back with minimal transition. Long rest only after B3.
Giant Set
B1DB Pullover
B2Barbell Floor Press
B3DB Bent-Over Row
C
Full Body Finish — Circuit
C1→C2→C3→C4→C5→C6, minimal rest between moves. 3 rounds total.
Circuit
C1BB Overhead Press
C2DB Lateral Raises
C3BB Glute Bridges
C4BB / DB Bicep Curls
C5DB Floor Skullcrushers
C6DB Reverse Flyes
📈
Progression Rule
After every session, log weight and reps for Set 2 and Set 3 of each block. Next week: aim for 1 extra rep at the same load, or add a small weight increment at the same reps. Always harder than last time.
